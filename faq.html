Questions

Before You Enter.

Answers to the most common questions about The Iron.

The Iron is a private development community for men built around seven areas: Mind, Character, Body, Money, Career, Relationships and Purpose. It combines structure, education, accountability and community to help men become more capable and build lives they respect.
Men who want to become more capable and are willing to take responsibility for their development. Whether you're just starting out or already on your journey, The Iron is designed to meet you where you are. You don't need to have your life figured out — you just need to be serious about becoming better.
The Iron Open is the free community — and the front door to The Iron. It's a place for men to experience the culture, do real work, and see whether The Iron is right for them. It includes community access, the weekly standard, the weekly question, weekly wins and accountability, selected educational content, and access to the Iron 7 challenge. It's free forever.
The Iron Membership includes everything in Iron Open plus: full Iron Library access, the complete Iron Path framework, Iron 30 & Iron 90 challenges, masterclass recordings, member-only sessions, structured accountability, and advanced frameworks and exercises. It's the complete system.
The Iron Path is the framework that guides your development. Six stages — Foundation, Direction, Capability, Relationships, Leadership, Legacy — each building on the last. It's not a course. It's a progression.
The Inner Circle is a smaller environment designed for deeper accountability, feedback and interaction. It includes everything in The Iron Membership plus a private community, small-group sessions, scheduled founder sessions, hot seats, 90-day development planning, deeper feedback and limited membership. It launches separately.
The Iron is not a religious organisation. The phrase "Iron sharpens iron" is part of the philosophy and reflects the idea that people improve through meaningful relationships, challenge and community. The community welcomes men from all backgrounds.
