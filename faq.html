The Iron is a private development community for men built around six areas: Mind, Character, Body, Relationships, Money and Purpose. It provides structure, education, accountability and community for men who want to become more capable.

The Iron membership includes everything in Foundation plus: full education library, complete development paths, advanced resources, masterclass recordings, member-only sessions, structured accountability, advanced challenges, and practical frameworks and exercises.
The Inner Circle is a smaller environment designed for deeper accountability, feedback and interaction.
