The Iron Framework

Six Plans of Development

Each plan is a chapter in your development. Start anywhere.
Build in any order. The framework is designed to be flexible.

"Do not conform to the pattern of this world,
but be transformed by the renewing of your mind." — Romans 12:2
01

Mind

Master yourself.

Mental strength. Emotional regulation. Discipline. Confidence. Resilience. Develop the ability to think clearly, regulate your emotions, stay disciplined and remain resilient when circumstances become difficult.

Mental strength Emotional regulation Discipline Confidence Resilience Self-awareness
Explore →
02

Character

Become someone you respect.

Integrity. Responsibility. Courage. Leadership. Standards. Character is what remains when nobody is watching. Build integrity, responsibility, courage, confidence and the ability to keep your word.

Integrity Responsibility Courage Leadership Standards Self-respect
Explore →
03

Body

Take care of what carries you.

Strength. Nutrition. Recovery. Health. Your body is not the whole of your life. But it carries you through all of it. Build strength, health, physical discipline and habits that increase your capacity.

Strength Training Nutrition Sleep Health Recovery
Explore →
04

Relationships

Build connections that matter.

Communication. Boundaries. Trust. Connection. The quality of your relationships shapes the quality of your life. Develop communication, emotional maturity, boundaries, trust and the ability to build relationships without losing yourself.

Communication Boundaries Emotional maturity Connection Respect Trust
Explore →
05

Money

Become financially capable.

Income. Saving. Investing. Ownership. Money is not the purpose of life. But financial capability gives you options, responsibility and greater control over the direction of your life. Learn to earn, manage, save and invest with discipline.

Financial literacy Income Saving Investing Career Ownership
Explore →
06

Purpose

Find something worth building.

Direction. Skill. Work. Contribution. A life without direction eventually becomes a life dictated by circumstance. Develop useful skills, meaningful work and a reason to keep moving forward.

Direction Skills Work Meaning Contribution Purpose
