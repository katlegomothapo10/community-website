Overview

BUILD WEALTH THAT LASTS

Money is not the goal — but it is a tool. With money, you have choices. Without it, you're limited. The goal is to build enough wealth to live life on your terms.

The Money Plan teaches you financial literacy, how to save, invest, and build income. It's not about get-rich-quick — it's about building real, sustainable wealth over time.

You'll learn how to take control of your finances, build multiple income streams, and create a financial future that gives you freedom.

FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

WHAT'S INCLUDED IN THE MONEY PLAN?
The Money Plan includes six comprehensive modules covering Financial Literacy, Saving & Budgeting, Investing, Career & Income, Multiple Income Streams, and Wealth Building Principles. You get access to video lessons, exercises, community support, and financial tools.
IS THIS FOR BEGINNERS?
Yes. The Money Plan is designed for men at every stage of their financial journey — from complete beginners to those with some experience.
HOW DOES THE MONEY PLAN DIFFER BY TIER?
Free members get Module 1 (Financial Literacy Basics) and community access. Paid members get all 6 modules, live sessions, and accountability groups. VIP members get 1-on-1 coaching, personalized financial plans, and direct mentor access.
IS THIS FINANCIAL ADVICE?
No. The Money Plan provides educational content on financial literacy and wealth-building principles. It is not individualized financial advice or a guarantee of returns.
