What We Believe

Six Beliefs.

These are not slogans. They are the principles that guide every decision we make.

A man is responsible for his development.

Not everything that happens to him is his fault. But what he does next is his responsibility.

Strength is more than physical.

Mental strength. Emotional stability. Financial discipline. Physical health. Character. Relationships. Purpose.

Discipline beats motivation.

You shouldn't need to feel inspired to do what matters. Build a life that doesn't depend on how you feel.

Men need other men.

Not competition. Not posturing. Not hierarchy for its own sake. Brotherhood.

Capability creates confidence.

Don't just tell a man to believe in himself. Help him become someone who has earned that belief.

Masculinity should produce responsibility.

Not entitlement. Not domination. Not resentment. Responsibility.

The Foundation

Character Under Everything.

The point isn't simply to become successful.
It's to become someone worth becoming.

Underneath The Six Areas.

You can develop your mind, body, money, career, relationships and purpose — and still become someone you don't respect.

Character is the foundation. It is what remains when nobody is watching. It is the relationship between what you believe and how you behave.

The Iron is not built around performance. It is built around the person you are becoming.
