Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

"Get clarity."
WHAT IS THE IRON?
The Iron is a private community for men who want to become more capable, disciplined, grounded and self-respecting. It's a framework, an environment and a community for building a life you respect. "Iron sharpens iron."
WHO IS THE IRON FOR?
Men approximately 18-40 who want greater discipline, direction, character, relationships, financial capability and purpose. Students, professionals, entrepreneurs, creators — any man who wants to build.
DO I NEED TO HAVE LIFE FIGURED OUT?
Not at all. The Iron is for men who are works in progress. You don't need to have everything figured out — you need to be willing to build.
IS THIS ONLY FOR MEN?
The Iron is primarily designed for men, with content and community structured around masculine development. Women are welcome and have found value in the community, but the focus is on men's growth.
WHAT DOES "IRON SHARPENS IRON" MEAN?
It's from Proverbs 27:17 — "Iron sharpens iron, so one man sharpens another." It means men grow best in community with other men who challenge, encourage and hold them accountable.
WHAT DO I GET FOR FREE?
Community access, general discussion, selected education, challenges, wins, reflection tools, selected free events, and the ability to purchase individual masterclasses.
WHAT DO I GET WITH THE PAID MEMBERSHIP?
Everything in Free, plus full learning paths, premium education, masterclasses, member-only workshops, live sessions, accountability, premium resources, deeper discussions, and member-only challenges.
WHAT IS THE INNER CIRCLE?
A limited-access tier with smaller group size, private sessions, priority questions, deeper feedback, small-group accountability, direct founder access, and premium networking.
IS THIS FINANCIAL ADVICE?
No. All investing and financial content is educational and is not individualized financial advice or a guarantee of returns.
