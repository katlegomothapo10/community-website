03 / The Standard

How We Show Up.

Take responsibility.
Own your decisions.
Keep your word.
Especially the promises you make to yourself.
Respect people.
Strength doesn't require disrespect.
Challenge ideas, not people.
Disagree with rigour, not ego.
Don't pretend to be an expert.
Stay curious. Keep learning.
Leave people better than you found them.
The standard applies to how you treat others too.
