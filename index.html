The Standard

THE STANDARD

A better life isn't built by motivation.

It's built through the standards you set, the decisions you make and the actions you repeat.

The Iron isn't about perfection.

It's about becoming more capable than you were yesterday — and continuing to improve.

You don't need to become someone else.

You need to become more capable of being yourself.

"Raise the standard. Do the work. Build accordingly."

Who It's For

WHO IS THIS FOR

The Iron is for men who know they have more work to do.

You don't need to:

  • Have your life figured out
  • Be wealthy
  • Have an impressive physique
  • Have perfect discipline
  • Be successful
  • Know exactly what you want to do

You just need to:

  • Be willing to build

Your starting point doesn't determine your destination.

What you do next does.

The Code

THE IRON CODE

  • Take responsibility. Own your decisions.
  • Keep your word. Especially the promises you make to yourself.
  • Respect people. Strength doesn't require disrespect.
  • Don't pretend to be an expert. Stay curious. Keep learning.
  • Do the work. Knowledge without action changes very little.
  • Build quietly. Your development doesn't need to become a performance.
  • Leave people better. The standard applies to how you treat others too.
The Mission

WHY THE IRON EXISTS

There are plenty of places online telling men what they should be.

Be richer. Be stronger. Be more dominant. Get more women. Work harder. Never show weakness.

The Iron takes a different approach.

We believe becoming a better man isn't about performing an image.

It's about developing the capabilities that allow you to live a life you respect.

To think clearly. To control yourself. To keep your word. To take care of your body. To build healthy relationships. To become financially capable. To find direction.

And to keep improving.

"Not perfection. Progress with standards."

BUILD A LIFE YOU RESPECT.

You need to decide what needs to change. Then start.
