Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is The Iron?
The Iron is a private community for men who want to become more capable, disciplined, grounded and self-respecting. It's a framework, an environment and a community for building a life you respect.
What's the difference between Free and The Forge?
Free helps you understand. The Forge helps you implement. Free focuses on principles, education and community. The Forge provides detailed frameworks, systems, challenges, accountability and practical tools.
What is the Inner Circle?
The Inner Circle is the future premium level of The Iron. It will focus on individualized guidance and accountability. It is currently coming soon and is not yet available.
Do I need to have my life together to join?
No. The Iron is for men who are works in progress. You don't need to arrive finished. You need to be willing to build.
Is The Iron only about masculinity?
No. Character and masculinity are part of the foundation, but The Iron addresses the whole man: his mind, character, body, relationships, money and purpose.
Is this a dating community?
No. Relationships and dating are one pillar. The objective is to build a complete life.
Do I need to participate every day?
No. The goal is to improve your real life — not spend your entire life inside a community.
Is this financial advice?
No. All investing and financial content is educational and is not individualized financial advice or a guarantee of returns.
