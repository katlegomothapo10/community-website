Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

WHAT IS THE BLUEPRINT?
The Blueprint is a private community for men who want to become more capable, disciplined, grounded and self-respecting. It's a framework, an environment and a community for building a life you respect.
WHO IS THE BLUEPRINT FOR?
Men approximately 18-40 who want greater discipline, direction, character, relationships, financial capability and purpose. Students, professionals, entrepreneurs, creators — anyone who wants to build.
DO I NEED TO HAVE LIFE FIGURED OUT?
Not at all. The Blueprint is for men who are works in progress. You don't need to have everything figured out — you need to be willing to build.
IS THIS ONLY FOR MEN?
The Blueprint is primarily designed for men, with content and community structured around the development of masculine identity. While women are welcome, the community's focus is on men's development.
WHAT DO I GET FOR FREE?
Community access, general discussion, selected education, challenges, wins, reflection tools, selected free events, and the ability to purchase individual masterclasses.
WHAT DO I GET WITH THE PAID MEMBERSHIP?
Everything in Free, plus full learning paths, premium education, masterclasses, member-only workshops, live sessions, accountability, premium resources, deeper discussions, and member-only challenges.
WHAT IS THE INNER CIRCLE?
A limited-access tier with smaller group size, private sessions, priority questions, deeper feedback, small-group accountability, direct founder access, and premium networking.
WHERE DOES THE COMMUNITY LIVE?
The community is hosted on a private platform. This website is the front door — the community itself is where the building happens.
IS THIS FINANCIAL ADVICE?
No. All investing and financial content is educational and is not individualized financial advice or a guarantee of returns.
