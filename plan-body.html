The Framework

THE BODY MODULES

Six modules covering every aspect of building a strong, healthy body.

MODULE 01

Training & Strength

Learn how to build muscle, burn fat, and develop functional strength. From proper form to progressive overload, you'll master the fundamentals of effective training.

MODULE 02

Nutrition & Fuel

Learn what to eat, when to eat, and how to fuel your body for performance and health. No fad diets — just real, sustainable nutrition.

MODULE 03

Sleep & Recovery

Optimize your sleep and recovery. This is where the real growth happens. Learn how to sleep better, recover faster, and perform at your best.

MODULE 04

Health & Wellness

Build a healthy, resilient body that can withstand stress and keep going. Learn about hormone health, stress management, and long-term wellness.

MODULE 05

Grooming & Presentation

Take pride in your appearance. Learn how to groom, dress, and present yourself with confidence. How you look matters — and so does how you feel.

MODULE 06

Physical Discipline

Build the discipline to train consistently, eat well, and take care of your body every day. This is the module that makes everything else stick.

FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What's included in the Body Plan?
The Body Plan covers training, nutrition, sleep, health, grooming and physical discipline. The depth of access depends on your membership level.
Do I need a gym?
No. The Body Plan includes both gym-based and home-based workout options. Whether you have a full gym or just a set of dumbbells, you can follow the program.
How does the Body Plan differ by tier?
Free introduces the principles and community. The Iron provides the full educational experience, practical frameworks, exercises, challenges and accountability. The Inner Circle adds deeper accountability, feedback and personal development support.
Is this for beginners?
Yes. The Body Plan is designed for all levels — from complete beginners to advanced athletes. There are modifications and progressions for every exercise and program.
